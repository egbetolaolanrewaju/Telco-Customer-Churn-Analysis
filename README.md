# Telco Customer Churn & Retention Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Telco Customer Churn & Retention Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes customer retention and churn patterns across different customer segments, tenure groups, contract types, internet services, and payment methods.

The goal of the analysis is to identify high-risk customer segments and provide actionable insights that can support customer retention strategies and reduce churn.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions:

- What percentage of customers are leaving the company?
- How many customers have been retained versus churned?
- Which customer tenure groups have the highest churn rates?
- Which contract types are associated with higher churn?
- Which internet services have higher customer churn?
- Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?
- Which customer segments should receive targeted retention strategies?

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 7,043 |

The dashboard shows that approximately **26.54% of customers have churned**, highlighting a significant customer retention challenge.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Churn Rate by Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure have substantially higher churn rates.

| Tenure Group | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| 0–12 Months | 47.44% |
| 13–24 Months | 28.71% |
| 25–48 Months | 20.39% |
| 49–72 Months | 9.51% |

The analysis indicates that newer customers are significantly more likely to leave the company than long-term customers.

---

### 2. Churn Rate by Contract Type

Contract type is one of the strongest churn indicators in the analysis.

| Contract Type | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-month | 42.71% |
| One year | 11.27% |
| Two year | 2.83% |

Customers on month-to-month contracts show substantially higher churn than customers on longer-term contracts.

---

### 3. Churn Rate by Internet Service

Churn varies considerably by internet service:

- Fiber optic: **41.89%**
- DSL: **18.96%**
- No internet service: **7.40%**

Fiber-optic customers represent a particularly important segment for further investigation and retention analysis.

---

### 4. Churn Rate by Payment Method

Electronic check customers show the highest churn rate among the payment methods analyzed.

| Payment Method | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|
| Electronic check | 45.29% |
| Mailed check | 19.11% |
| Bank transfer (automatic) | 16.71% |
| Credit card (automatic) | 15.24% |

This provides an opportunity to investigate whether payment behavior is associated with customer retention.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis identified several important patterns:

- Overall customer churn is **26.54%**.
- Customers with **0–12 months of tenure** have the highest churn rate at **47.44%**.
- **Month-to-month customers** have a very high churn rate of **42.71%**.
- **Fiber-optic customers** have a churn rate of **41.89%**.
- Customers paying through **electronic checks** have the highest churn rate at **45.29%**.
- Customers with longer-term contracts have significantly lower churn rates.
- Customer retention improves considerably as customer tenure increases.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies could help reduce customer churn:

### 1. Focus on New Customers

Customers within their first 12 months should receive stronger onboarding and engagement programs.

### 2. Encourage Longer-Term Contracts

The company could offer incentives or discounts to encourage month-to-month customers to move to one-year or two-year contracts.

### 3. Investigate Fiber-Optic Churn

The high churn rate among fiber-optic customers warrants further investigation into pricing, service quality, customer expectations, and technical support.

### 4. Improve Payment Experience

The company could investigate why electronic-check customers have substantially higher churn and encourage customers to adopt automatic payment methods where appropriate.

### 5. Build Customer Risk Segmentation

Customers combining multiple high-risk characteristics could be identified and prioritized for proactive retention campaigns.
